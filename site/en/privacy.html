TripReady helps you organize what you need to prepare before traveling. To build a checklist that makes sense for your trip, the app needs to know very little about you — and this policy explains exactly what, why, and what you can do about it.

1. Who is responsible for your data

TripReady is offered by {{RAZAO_SOCIAL}}, registered under CNPJ number {{CNPJ}}, which acts as the controller of the personal data processed in the app.

For any privacy-related matter, including exercising your rights, the contact channel is {{EMAIL_CONTATO}}.

2. What data TripReady processes

Your account data

  • Display name, used to personalize the app.
  • Email address, which identifies your account.
  • Profile photo, when you sign in with Google and it is made available. If you sign in with Apple, there is no photo and the app shows a default avatar.
  • Password, only if you create the account with email and password. It is stored encrypted by the authentication service and is not readable by us.
  • An internal identifier that links your information to your account.

Data you enter to build the checklist

  • The passport country you use to travel, which one is your main passport when you register more than one, and which one you chose for each trip.
  • Your trips: the name you give each one, the dates, the countries and the cities chosen.
  • Your checklist: the items generated for the trip, the items you create yourself, and which of them you have marked as checked.

Use of partner recommendations

TripReady logs some events related to the use of app features, such as clicks on partner links, exclusively to measure the use of these recommendations and track the performance of affiliate links.

What is logged is the partner indicated, the recommendation you tapped, the trip from which it happened, and the moment of the tap. Nothing you filled in the checklist goes along with this record, and the partner does not receive any information about you.

Data stored on your device

So you do not have to sign in again every time you open the app, TripReady keeps your session credentials on the device itself. They are not sent to anyone and disappear when you sign out.

What TripReady does not collect

This is worth spelling out, because it is a product choice, not an oversight. The app does not collect:

  • passport number;
  • passport expiration date;
  • photo or copy of the passport;
  • biometric data;
  • health information;
  • religion;
  • political opinion;
  • sexual orientation;
  • any other sensitive personal data;
  • your location;
  • advertising identifiers.

TripReady does not track your behavior outside the app, does not use your data for behavioral advertising, and does not build advertising profiles. Inside the app, the only usage record is the one described above, about partner recommendations. If this ever changes, this policy will be updated before the change goes live.

About the passport country: it indicates which document you use to travel and is what lets us know which entry rules apply to you. It is treated as common personal data.

3. How TripReady obtains this data

  • When you sign in with Google or Apple. Authentication happens in your device's browser or in the system's own screen, and TripReady never has access to your password for these services. We only receive your name, email address, and, in Google's case, your photo. If you choose to hide your email when signing in with Apple, we receive the relay address Apple creates for you.
  • When you create an account with email and password. In this case we send a message to the address provided, with a confirmation link, to make sure the email is really yours. The account is activated when you confirm it.
  • When you use the app. You are the one who chooses the passport country, creates trips, and checks off checklist items.
  • When the app builds your checklist. The items are generated from the passport you chose for that trip, the destinations, and the period.

4. What the data is used for

  • Email and password — identify your account, allow sign-in, and recover access.
  • Name and photo — personalize the app for you.
  • Passport country — determine which entry rules apply to you at each destination.
  • Trips, dates and destinations — build the right checklist and show what stage each trip is at.
  • Checklist items — keep track of what has already been checked and calculate the trip's progress.
  • Session on the device — keep you signed in between one opening and the next.

These uses are necessary to provide the service you requested when you created your account. TripReady does not use your data for advertising, to build behavioral profiles, or for any purpose other than the ones described here.

5. How long the data is kept

While your account exists, your trips and your checklist remain available to you — including trips already completed, which stay in your history until you delete them.

When you delete your account, the data is permanently deleted at the moment of deletion. There is no backup from which it can be recovered afterward.

The only exception is the count of clicks on partner recommendations: the record stops being linked to you and your trip, and remains only as a number added to that partner's total. From that point on, it does not identify anyone.

If you create an account and never confirm your email, the registration stays on record, with no trip data, until you complete it or request removal through the contact channel.

Technical operation and security logs kept by the infrastructure that hosts the service follow the policies of the provider described in section 7.

6. Where the data is stored

The information in your account and your trips is kept on servers operated by Supabase, located in the United States. This means there is an international transfer of your personal data.

The company responsible for TripReady remains the controller of this information and is accountable for it, regardless of where it is stored.

7. Who the data is shared with

TripReady does not sell your personal data and does not share it with commercial partners.

There are only the following parties involved, each with a specific role:

  • Supabase — stores the information and handles authentication, acting as a processor on TripReady's behalf. It is also the service that sends the sign-up confirmation and password reset messages.
  • Google and Apple — only when you choose to sign in through them. In that case, the provider knows you authenticated with TripReady because you authorized it. When the app displays your Google photo, it is loaded directly from Google's servers.
  • App Store and Google Play — distribute the app, under each store's policies.

We may also share information when required by law or by order of a competent authority, to the extent required.

8. Links to other sites

Some checklist items include links, and they are of two kinds:

  • Official sources — the page of the immigration authority or foreign ministry that sets the requirement.
  • Suggestions of where to resolve the item — for example, travel insurance or eSIM providers.

Some of these suggestions are affiliate links, which means TripReady may receive a commission if you purchase through them, at no additional cost to you. This commission does not influence what the checklist says you need to check: the requirement comes from the official source, and the suggestion is only where to resolve it.

In all cases, the link opens in your device's browser and TripReady does not send any of your data to these sites. What they see is what any visit to a page reveals, such as your IP address. From the moment you leave the app, each site's own privacy policy applies.

The count of how many people tapped each suggestion is done by TripReady itself, to know which recommendations are useful. It stays with us and is not sent to the partner.

If we ever start sending any of your information to a partner — for example, for a quote requested inside the app —, this policy will be updated beforehand.

9. Security

TripReady treats the protection of your data as part of the product, not as an implementation detail:

  • each person only accesses their own information, and this rule is enforced at the database level, not only on the screens;
  • the app does not carry any key capable of accessing other people's data;
  • authentication uses the secure flows offered by Google, Apple, and the authentication service, and your password never travels into the app;
  • account deletion can only reach the account of the person who requested it.

No service can promise absolute security, but we maintain measures appropriate to the kind of information processed and review these measures as the product evolves.

10. How to delete your account and your data

You can delete everything without having to talk to anyone:

Profile → Delete account. The app asks you to type a confirmation word and, once you finish, permanently removes your profile, your passports, your trips, the destinations, and the checklists. The action is irreversible and leaves no copy.

You can also delete just one trip, from its own screen. In that case, the destinations and the checklist for that trip are deleted along with it, and the rest of your account stays as it is.

  • request the portability of your data;
  • be informed about who we share your data with;
  • withdraw consent, when the processing is based on it.

Deleting your account you can do on your own, right in the app, as described in section 10. For any other request, write to {{EMAIL_CONTATO}}: we confirm receipt, verify that the request comes from the account holder, and respond within the timeframe set by law.

12. Use by minors

TripReady is intended for people 16 years of age or older and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone below that age. If we identify a registration under these conditions, or if a guardian notifies us through the contact channel, the account and the associated data will be removed.
