1. What TripReady is

TripReady is a trip organization app. It gathers, in a checklist, what you need to check before traveling, based on the passport you chose for that trip, the destinations, and the period — and shows, whenever available, the link to the official source of each requirement.

What TripReady is not, and this is important to make clear:

  • it does not provide legal advice;
  • it does not issue, apply for, or act as an intermediary for visas or documents;
  • it does not guarantee your entry into any country.

The information in the app is guidance to help you prepare. The final word always belongs to the official source and the authority of the destination country.

2. How the checklist works

Your checklist items are built from a catalog of rules, cross-referencing the passport you chose for the trip, the countries, the period, and, in some cases, whether the destination belongs to a group of countries with common rules.

Each trip has its own passport. When you change it, the checklist is reviewed: items that still apply keep what you had already checked, and items that no longer apply stay visible in a separate section instead of disappearing. Removing a passport from your profile does not change any trip — upcoming and ongoing ones start asking you to choose another, and completed ones stay exactly as they are.

Each item has a priority:

  • Required — counts toward whether the trip is ready;
  • Recommended — contributes to your progress;
  • Informational — shown for you to know, without affecting progress.

Two points worth understanding:

  • The checklist is a snapshot of the moment it was created. If a rule changes afterward, the items already generated for your trip do not rewrite themselves.
  • You can create your own items, which do not come from the catalog and are solely your responsibility.

4. How current the information is

The catalog's entry rules are recorded from official sources and keep the date they were verified. Even so, immigration rules change without prior notice, and there may be a gap between a change and the app's update.

TripReady does not promise that all content is updated in real time, and that is why it insists, on every item, that you confirm through official channels.

5. Travel and immigration requirements

The catalog's coverage also has limits: depending on the passport registered, the app may not yet have specific visa rules for every destination. When there is no visa item on your checklist, this does not mean the visa is waived — it means you need to check directly with the official source.
