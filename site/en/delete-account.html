Delete my account

You can delete your TripReady account and all the data linked to it. Deletion is permanent and irreversible.

From the app

This is the fastest way, and does not depend on anyone else:

  • open TripReady and go to Profile;
  • tap Delete account, at the bottom of the screen;
  • type the confirmation word and finish.

The account is deleted right away.

Without the app

If you have already uninstalled it or cannot sign in, write to {{EMAIL_CONTATO}} requesting deletion, from the account's email address. We confirm the request comes from the account holder and complete the deletion.

What gets deleted

  • the account and sign-in data;
  • the profile;
  • the registered passports;
  • the trips, with destinations and dates;
  • the checklists and the items checked off.

What stays on record

Nothing. We do not keep a backup this data can be recovered from afterward, so there is no retention period to observe: deletion is final the moment it happens.

Details about how your data is handled are in the Privacy Policy.
